# Edit a poem where you read it

Open a poem in the protected preview or this checkout’s local development site.
Choose **Edit poem**, then click or tap the words you want to change. The title
is editable too, when the poem displays one.

- **Enter** adds a line; **Tab** inserts indentation. Use **Shift Tab** to leave
  the text with the keyboard.
- Select words and choose **I** or **B** for emphasis. Pasted text keeps its
  characters and spacing, without importing formatting from another app.
- **Save** keeps the edit for your next visit. **Cancel** restores the poem as
  it was when you started. **⌘ S / Ctrl S** also saves.
- The curved arrows undo/redo typing while you edit. After saving, **Undo**
  and **Redo** reverse saved changes made during that visit.

If a save fails, your words stay on the page. If another tab saved first, the
editor tells you and keeps your unsaved version. Copy any lines you want to keep
before choosing **Load saved version**.

Site edits are saved separately from Notion. They do not rewrite the original
Notion draft, source links, attribution, or import snapshots. The page warns you
if its imported source changes after you made site edits.

Local saves work on `http://127.0.0.1:5211`. The hosted preview needs its approved
private storage connection before **Save** is enabled. The public production
site keeps its current reading experience until a separate release is approved.
